# D&D mechanics: what is modeled and what is missing

SRD 5.1 only. "Modeled" means the engine decides it; "narrative" means the sheet names it but no rule reads it.
Race and subrace traits are tracked separately in `dnd-race-mechanics-status.md`.

## Modeled

- Combat: initiative, actions, bonus actions, reactions, movement and zones, opportunity attacks, advantage and disadvantage, death saves, concentration.
- Monsters: Multiattack, breath weapons with recharge, Regeneration, Legendary Resistance, legendary actions (weapon attacks only), auras (Frightful Presence), spellcasting.
- Spells: all 319 SRD spells exist; 294 are mechanical, and every caster has a spellbook.
- Gear: 187 pieces of equipment, tools, mounts and vehicles.
- Classes: Second Wind, Sneak Attack, Lay on Hands, Disciple of Life, Divine Smite, Extra Attack (2 to 4 attacks), Cunning Action, Uncanny Dodge, Wild Shape (limits by level), Rage, Champion, Draconic Bloodline.

## Missing

### Spells
- Reaction spells other than Shield (Counterspell, Hellish Rebuke, Absorb Elements...).
- Spells that need terrain, teleporting or summoned creatures.

### Monsters
- Legendary actions that are not attacks (Wing Attack, Detect...).
- Possession and charm gazes.
- Engulf and swallow.

### Class features
- Rage: no early end when the barbarian neither attacks nor is hit, no spellcasting block, uses do not grow past 2.
- Subclasses: only Champion and Draconic Bloodline are mechanical. Missing: Berserker Frenzy, Thief, Lore, Open Hand, Hunter, Devotion, Fiend, Evocation.
- Bardic Inspiration (feature actions can only target the user).
- Ki and Martial Arts.
- Fighting styles other than Dueling; Action Surge; Indomitable.
- Level 2+ features of most classes are narrative only.

### Items
- Magic items (needs the SRD magic items file; not downloaded).
- Attunement, charges, cursed items.

### Other
- Zh-TW names for monsters, items and spells are machine-assisted and need a human read.
- Live Discord checks only the user can do.
