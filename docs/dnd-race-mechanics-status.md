# SRD 5.1 race mechanics status

The character builder targets the nine playable races in the 2014 SRD 5.1. New builds select explicit Hill/Mountain Dwarf, High/Wood/Drow Elf, Lightfoot/Stout Halfling, Forest/Rock Gnome, or one of the ten Dragonborn ancestries. Human, Half-Elf, Half-Orc, and Tiefling remain direct choices. The old broad Elf, Dwarf, Halfling, Gnome, and Dragonborn IDs remain valid for saved characters and imports but are no longer offered to new builds.

Mechanical choices now represented: racial ability score increases; Half-Elf's two selected +1 increases and two Skill Versatility proficiencies; speed (including Wood Elf's 35 feet); Elf Keen Senses' Perception proficiency; Hill Dwarf's extra hit point at creation and every level; and Dwarf, Stout Halfling, Dragonborn ancestry, and Tiefling damage resistances. They are derived from the saved build and applied by the same character/combat paths as class bonuses. Portrait and display names use the selected race's content ID.

This is **not full SRD racial rules fidelity**. The engine still has no lighting/vision or languages and tools system, so Darkvision, language grants, tool proficiencies, and related traits have no effect. Now modeled as well: advantage on saves against being charmed (Fey Ancestry, Half-Elf), frightened (Halfling Brave), poison (Dwarven Resilience, Stout Resilience) and magic on Int/Wis/Cha saves (Gnome Cunning); Half-Orc Savage Attacks and Relentless Endurance (once per long rest). Still missing: Halfling Lucky, Elf Trance and subrace magic, Dragonborn Breath Weapon, Gnome tinkering and subrace magic, Tiefling Infernal Legacy. Drow Sunlight Sensitivity also needs lighting context. Mountain Dwarf armor training cannot matter while the engine treats a hero as proficient with every carried item. The broader set of races published outside SRD 5.1 is outside this catalog.

Before claiming full SRD race compatibility, implement those shared systems and add combat/exploration tests for each trait. Keep the legacy race IDs readable while evolving the saved build schema.
